Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Q

Is the game really free?

A

Practice, the 10-level Challenge and mistake review are free — no account, no ads, no tracking. Live Race multiplayer and custom printable worksheets are part of Supporter. You can try the full Supporter Edition for 7 days for $1.

Q

What is included in a family membership?

A

One membership covers up to 2 parents and 2 children: Live Race multiplayer, the custom worksheet generator (4 difficulty ranges, 5–100 questions, US Letter/A4, optional answer page), and every future Supporter feature. All plans unlock the same features.

Q

How much does the Supporter Edition cost?

A

$4.99 per month, $49 per year (about $4.08 per month — $10.88 less than twelve monthly payments), or $149 once for lifetime access.

Q

How does the $1 7-day trial work?

A

Sign in with a parent account and pay $1 once. Your family gets 7 days of full Supporter access — Live Race and custom worksheets included. It does not renew automatically and is limited to one trial per family. When the 7 days end, paid features lock automatically and nothing else is charged.

Q

What happens after the 7-day trial?

A

Live Race and custom worksheets lock. Practice, Challenge, mistake review, your child profiles and all learning data stay. You can subscribe at any time to unlock the paid features again.

Q

Do the monthly and yearly plans auto-renew? Can I cancel?

A

Yes — they renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel from your Parent Center at any time, and your access continues until the end of the period you have paid for. The Lifetime plan is a single payment and never renews.

Q

Do I need a parent account? How does my child sign in?

A

You do not need an account to play Practice or Challenge. The membership and child logins live on a parent account. In the Parent Center, a parent creates up to 2 child accounts with a username and password (no email). Children choose Student sign-in, then play on their own device.

Q

How do I add another parent?

A

The family owner can invite one more parent by email from the Parent Center. They sign in with that email or Google and join the same family, sharing the same membership. Each family has 2 parent seats and 2 child seats.

Q

How do I buy it, and who handles the payment?

A

Choose a plan above; checkout opens on Waffo Pancake's secure hosted page. Waffo acts as our merchant of record, so card details and sales tax are handled there — we only receive the order result and never see or store your card number. Monthly and yearly renewals can be cancelled anytime in the Parent Center; the one-time Lifetime purchase never renews.

Q

What if it doesn't work the way we describe?

A

Open Manage subscription & refunds in the Parent Center to request a refund, or write to hi@tangyuanmath.com. We'll fix it or refund you.

Q

Are there download limits?

A

To keep generation fast for everyone, each family is limited to 20 worksheet downloads per 10 minutes — far more than a family needs in a day.

Q

What happens if Tangyuan Math ever stops?

A

A subscription or lifetime purchase is not a promise that the service runs forever. If we ever have to stop it, we'll announce it ahead of time and treat paid supporters fairly.

Q

Still have a question?

A

Email hi@tangyuanmath.com — a human replies.
