Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Q

Is the game really free?

A

Yes. Untimed Practice, the 10-level Challenge, Live Race multiplayer and mistake review are free — no account, no ads, no tracking. The Supporter Edition only adds printable worksheet generation; the game itself is complete without it.

Q

How much does the Supporter Edition cost?

A

$4.99 per month, $49.90 per year (about $4.16 per month — $9.98 less than twelve monthly payments), or $149.99 once for lifetime access. All three plans unlock exactly the same Supporter features.

Q

What exactly does the Supporter Edition unlock?

A

A worksheet generator that creates brand-new printable PDFs on demand: four difficulty ranges, 5–100 questions, US Letter or A4, and an optional separate answer page. The fixed sample worksheets stay free for everyone.

Q

Do the monthly and yearly plans auto-renew? Can I cancel?

A

Yes — they renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel from your Parent Center at any time, and your access continues until the end of the period you have paid for. The Lifetime plan is a single payment and never renews.

Q

Do I need a parent account?

A

Not to play — the game works fully as a guest. The Supporter Edition is tied to a parent account so one subscription or purchase covers the whole family; you can set one up in the Parent Center.

Q

How do I buy it, and who handles the payment?

A

Online checkout is being activated. Until it is live, email hi@tangyuanmath.com with the plan you'd like and we'll set it up on your parent account right away. Payments are processed by our payment service provider — we never see or store your card details, only the order result.

Q

What if it doesn't work the way we describe?

A

Write to hi@tangyuanmath.com and we'll fix it or refund you.

Q

Are there download limits?

A

To keep generation fast for everyone, supporter downloads are limited to 20 per 10 minutes — far more than a family needs in a day.

Q

What happens if Tangyuan Math ever stops?

A

A subscription or lifetime purchase is not a promise that the service runs forever. If we ever have to stop it, we'll announce it ahead of time and treat paid supporters fairly.

Q

Still have a question?

A

Email hi@tangyuanmath.com — a human replies.
