Short version

Tangyuan Math is free to play and needs no account. Practice freely, and feel free to use it with family or students. Optional extras — a free parent account and the paid Supporter Edition — are described below. Don't resell, copy, or misuse our name.

What you may do

  • Use it free for personal learning, family practice, or classroom support
  • Share links to the site — everyone is welcome

What you may not do

  • Resell, rent out, or bundle the content into another paid product
  • Copy or scrape the game content in bulk, or publish under our name
  • Interfere with, damage, or try to bypass how the site works

Accounts (optional)

Parent accounts are entirely optional: the game works fully without one. By creating an account you agree to: not share it, not impersonate anyone, and not upload unlawful or harmful content. We may suspend or delete accounts that are abused.

Paid features — the Supporter Edition

  • The Supporter Edition (launch price US$4.99) is a one-time purchase attached to your parent account. There is no subscription and no automatic renewal.
  • It unlocks custom printable worksheet PDF generation (choice of difficulty, question count, US Letter/A4 paper, optional answer pages). Fair-use limits apply — currently 20 downloads per 10 minutes per account.
  • The final price, including any taxes, is always shown before you pay. Payments are processed by our third-party payment service provider; we never receive or store your card details, only the order result needed to keep your features unlocked.
  • Refunds: if a supporter feature doesn't work as described, contact hi@tangyuanmath.com and we will fix it or refund the purchase.
  • A one-time purchase is not a promise of perpetual service. If we ever have to stop the service, we will announce it ahead of time and handle paid entitlements fairly.
  • Refunding or revoking a purchase removes only what that purchase granted; your free access to the game is unaffected.
