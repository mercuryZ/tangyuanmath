Accounts (optional)

Parent accounts are optional for free practice. A parent account is required for the Supporter Edition and for child logins. By creating an account you agree to: not share it, not impersonate anyone, and not upload unlawful or harmful content. We may suspend or delete accounts that are abused.

Child accounts are created and controlled by a parent. They use a username and password, never an email address, and are linked to the family in our database. Parents are responsible for their child's account, can reset its password, and can delete it at any time.
