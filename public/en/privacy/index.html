Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What Tangyuan Math is

Tangyuan Math is a free, friendly web game that helps learners practice multiplication (up to two-digit × two-digit) through untimed practice, a 10-level challenge (plus a level-0 warm-up), and mistake review. It works in English and 中文. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

The app is published at https://tangyuanmath.com. We are the data controller for the account data described below, and you can reach us at hi@tangyuanmath.com. This policy covers the website, the game, and the optional parent accounts.

Short version

The game works without any account: most people play entirely in their browser, and their practice data never leaves their device. Parents may optionally create an account (with an email and password, or by signing in with Google) to sync a child's learning data across devices. We have no ads, no analytics, and no third-party scripts, and we never sell personal data.

What we collect

1. Playing as a guest (no account)

Everything stays in your browser's local storage and is never sent to our servers. The game stores:

  • tm_records — your recent practice rounds (latest 100, kept up to one year)
  • tm_wrongbook — questions you got wrong, for review
  • tm_settings — your preferences (language, animations)
  • tm_lang, tm_challenge_level — language choice and challenge progress

2. Parent account (email & password)

If you create an account, our server stores:

  • Your email address (used to sign in and to send verification / password-reset links)
  • A hashed password (we never store the plain text)
  • Your language and timezone preferences
  • Your account creation date and your family membership (which family you belong to and your role in it)
  • If you add them: child accounts (a nickname, chosen avatar, and — if you create one — a username and hashed password), their last sign-in time, and their synced practice records and mistake book
  • If you invite another parent: the invited email address, an invite token (stored as a hash) and its expiry status

Child accounts are created and controlled by a parent. They have a username and password instead of an email address, are linked to the family in our database, and never appear publicly. A parent can reset the password or delete the child account at any time.

3. Parent account (Sign in with Google)

When you choose Sign in with Google, we request only the minimal openid and email scopes. Our server receives only:

  • Your email address (Google has verified it belongs to you)
  • A Google account ID — enough to recognize you next time

We request no other profile data (no name, photo, contacts, or calendar). The same optional child profiles and synced learning data described above may be stored in your account.

4. Payments (Supporter Edition purchases)

If you buy the Supporter Edition, the payment itself is processed by Waffo Pancake, our payment provider and merchant of record, on their own secure checkout page. We never receive or store your card number or other payment credentials. What our server receives and stores with your account is limited to the order result: an order number, the amount, the currency, and the purchase status — just enough to keep your supporter features unlocked and for accounting. The billing portal in the Parent Center is hosted by Waffo Pancake; the data you enter there (such as a refund reason) is handled under Waffo's own privacy policy.

How we use the data

  • To provide the game — remembering progress, preferences, and mistake review
  • To sign you in — recognizing you and syncing learning data across your devices
  • To send account emails — email verification and password reset only
  • To process your purchases — keeping supporter features unlocked after a paid order
  • To support you — answering questions you send us

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking scripts
  • No visitor analytics or user profiling
  • No selling or renting of personal data to anyone
  • No third-party fonts or embeds — pages load only our own files
  • No AI image generation. Tangyuan Math is a math practice game. Google sign-in is used solely for authentication, and we do not use Google API data (or any data) to generate, create, train, or distribute AI-generated imagery — including non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) or any other synthetic media.

How Google data is used

Google data (your email address and Google account ID) is used only to sign you in and to associate your account with your child's synced learning data. It is not used for any other purpose, and it is not combined with any other Google data or disclosed to third parties. Sign-in happens on Google's own website (we don't embed Google scripts or buttons on our pages). You can review or remove our access anytime in your Google account permissions. Google's handling of your data is described in Google's Privacy Policy. Our use of data received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent personal data to any third party for their own purposes. Your data is stored only on our own server. Two services act strictly as processors when you choose to use them: Google acts solely as an identity provider during sign-in, and our payment service provider processes Supporter Edition payments (receiving only the data needed to complete the transaction). No advertising or analytics provider receives any data.

Where your data is stored

Account data is stored in a SQLite database on a persistent disk attached to our application server, hosted by Render (Render Services, Inc., United States). We do not use a separate analytics, advertising, or CRM provider. Because the server is located in the United States, data you provide may be transferred to and processed there; by creating an account you consent to that transfer. All traffic between your browser and our server is encrypted with HTTPS.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without an account. The game is fully usable as a guest; in that mode no data reaches our servers at all.
  • Access and export. In the Parent Center you can download a JSON export containing your account, child profiles, practice records, and mistake book.
  • Correct. You can change your language and timezone in the Parent Center at any time.
  • Delete. Deleting your account in the Parent Center permanently removes your email, Google link and synced data. If another parent remains in the family, the family and its child accounts stay with them; if you are the last parent, the entire family, child accounts and synced data are deleted. This cannot be undone.
  • Object or ask questions. Write to hi@tangyuanmath.com and we will respond.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our legal basis for processing account data is the performance of our agreement with you (providing the sync feature) and your consent, which you may withdraw at any time by deleting your account.

Data retention & deletion

  • Guest data stays in your browser and is deleted when you clear your browser data, or anytime in the game via Settings → Clear all my data.
  • Account data is kept until you delete it. You can export your data (JSON) and delete your account in the Parent Center; deleting your account removes your email, Google link, child profiles, and synced data from our server.
  • Login sessions expire automatically after 7 days.
  • Backups. We keep encrypted database backups for up to 30 days. A record deleted from the live service may remain in a backup until that backup expires, after which it is automatically purged.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as hashes (bcrypt). We request the minimum Google scopes needed for sign-in, and we keep no data longer than described above.

Children

Tangyuan Math is designed to be safe for young learners. Children are not required to provide any personal information: child accounts are created by a parent, use a username and password (never an email address), and are linked to the parent's family. A child's nickname and username are never shown publicly, and race display names are generated by the server. Nothing a child does is transmitted anywhere unless a parent chooses to create an account and sync data. Parent accounts are intended for parents or guardians. If you believe a child has created an account without a parent, contact us and we will delete it. We build with applicable children's-privacy rules (such as COPPA and GDPR) in mind.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at hi@tangyuanmath.com.
