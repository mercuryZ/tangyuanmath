Three ways to practice

Practice — untimed

Pick a number range (times tables up to two-digit × two-digit) and 5, 10 or 20 questions. Check answers whenever you're ready. Understanding first, speed later.

Challenge — 10 levels

10 questions per level, all correct to pass. Level 1 gives you 120 seconds; level 10 gives just 20. Optional speed goal for confident learners.

Mistake review

Wrong answers quietly collect in a mistake book. Replay them until they're gone — Tangyuan celebrates every one you clear.

Who it's for

Tangyuan Math is made for learners who already know their times tables and are ready for the next step: multiplying bigger numbers with confidence.

  • Families practicing at home
  • Homeschool parents looking for gentle drills
  • Teachers who need a warm-up or extra practice corner

Hints and step-by-step tips are built in — including six mental-math tricks inside the game. Speed is an option, never the only goal.
