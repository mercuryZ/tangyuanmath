A quick trick for multiplying 2-digit numbers

Here is a useful 2 digit multiplication trick. When the tens digits match and the units add to 10, multiply the tens digit by the next number for the front, then multiply the units for the back.

23 × 27 → front: 2 × 3 = 6, back: 3 × 7 = 21 → 621

The shortcut only works when both conditions hold. Otherwise, split one number into tens and ones. Our guide covers six mental-math tricks for multiplying 2-digit numbers, with worked examples and the conditions each one needs.

Why it's built this way

Untimed first, speed later

Practice has no clock by default. Understanding an answer beats racing it — children who aren't hurried tend to keep coming back, and coming back is what builds skill.

Mistakes come back — gently

Wrong answers collect in the mistake book and return until they're cleared. Meeting a fact again after a few days is how memory actually sticks.

Small, daily rounds

5 to 20 questions a sitting. A short round on a regular day beats a long drill on a reluctant one.
