Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Practice, the 10-level Challenge and mistake review are free — no account and no ads. Live Race multiplayer and custom printable worksheets are part of the Supporter Edition (from US$4.99/month, or US$149 once). You can try the full Supporter Edition for 7 days for $1. See pricing →

Do I need an account?

Not for Practice or Challenge — those work fully as a guest. A free parent account adds cloud sync, the Parent Center and family membership; it is required for Live Race and custom worksheets. Children sign in with their own username and password created by a parent.

What does a family membership include?

One Supporter membership covers up to 2 parents and 2 children. It unlocks Live Race multiplayer and the custom worksheet generator (4 difficulty ranges, 5–100 questions, US Letter/A4, optional answer page). You can try it for 7 days for $1; after that, Practice and Challenge remain free.

How does my child sign in?

A parent creates up to 2 child accounts in the Parent Center, each with a username and password (no email needed). Children choose Student sign-in on the Parent Center page, enter those details, and play on their own device.

How do I add another parent?

The family owner can invite one more parent by email from the Parent Center. They sign in with that email or Google and join the same family, sharing the same membership. Each family has 2 parent seats and 2 child seats.

Where is my data stored?

As a guest, everything stays in your own browser (local storage) and never leaves your device. With a parent or child account, practice records and the mistake book sync to our server so they follow you across devices. You can export or delete everything from the Parent Center.

Are there ads or trackers?

No ads, no analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts. The page loads everything from this site only.

Who is it for?

Learners who already know their times tables and want to get comfortable with bigger multiplication — at home, homeschool, or as classroom practice.

Which languages are supported?

English and Chinese (中文). Switch anytime from the top of the game screen.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes — it's designed for touch, keyboard and screen readers alike. Enter jumps to the next question on physical keyboards.

Will there be paid features?

There are now: the Supporter Edition includes Live Race multiplayer and custom worksheet generation (from US$4.99/month, or US$149 once). Free samples stay free, Practice and Challenge stay free, and we'll never add ads.

How do I buy the Supporter Edition?

Choose a plan on the pricing page; checkout opens on Waffo Pancake's secure hosted page. Waffo handles the card details and sales tax as our merchant of record — we only receive the order result. Monthly and yearly renewals can be cancelled anytime in the Parent Center.
