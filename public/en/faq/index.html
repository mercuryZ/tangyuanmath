❓ Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Playing is free: untimed Practice, the 10-level Challenge, Live Race multiplayer and mistake review all work without paying or registering. Two optional extras exist: a free parent account (sync progress across devices) and the paid Supporter Edition (US$4.99, one-time) that unlocks custom printable worksheet PDFs. See pricing →

Do I need an account?

No — the game (including multiplayer) works fully as a guest. A free parent account is optional and adds cloud sync, cross-device progress and the Parent Center; the Supporter Edition is tied to it.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser (local storage). Practice records, your mistake book and settings never leave your device. You can clear everything anytime in the game's Settings.

Are there ads or trackers?

No ads, no analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts. The page loads everything from this site only.

Who is it for?

Learners who already know their times tables and want to get comfortable with bigger multiplication — at home, homeschool, or as classroom practice.

Which languages are supported?

English and Chinese (中文). Switch anytime from the top of the game screen.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes — it's designed for touch, keyboard and screen readers alike. Enter jumps to the next question on physical keyboards.

Will there be paid features?

There are now: the Supporter Edition (US$4.99, one-time, no subscription) unlocks custom worksheet generation — free samples stay free. The free practice game stays complete on its own, and we'll never add ads.

How do I buy the Supporter Edition?

Online checkout is being activated. Until it's live, email hi@tangyuanmath.com and we'll enable it for your parent account right away.
