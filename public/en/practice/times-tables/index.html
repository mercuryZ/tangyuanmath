Times Tables Practice

Mixed facts from the multiplication tables, asked in random order — the classic drill, minus the clock. Choose the 1–10 tables for the school-year core or the 1–12 tables for the full chart, pick 5, 10 or 20 questions, and answer at whatever pace feels comfortable. The stopwatch is off by default and never ends a round.

This round is preset to your lesson's number range — the game opens with those exact facts selected, just press Start.

How a round works

  • Type the answer, press Enter. No multiple-choice guessing — the answer comes from memory or not at all. (The question below is tappable so you can feel the feedback first.)
  • Misses go to the mistake book. Every wrong answer is collected and quietly replayed in later rounds until it's answered right — spaced repetition without a spreadsheet.
  • Check answers whenever you like. In untimed practice you can review after every question or at the end of the round; nothing is scored until you say so.
  • Progress is private. As a guest everything stays in your own browser; a free parent account syncs it across devices.
