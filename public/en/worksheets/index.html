Worksheet FAQ

Are these multiplication worksheets really free?

Yes — the fixed sample PDFs are free for everyone, no account needed. The custom generator (pick difficulty, question count and paper size, then download a fresh PDF) is part of the Supporter Edition, which you can try for 3 days for $1.

Do the worksheets include answer pages?

Yes. Every worksheet PDF ends with an answer page printed on its own sheet — tick the box to include it or untick to skip it and save paper.

What is the difference between a worksheet and a times table printable chart?

A worksheet asks questions with space to write answers; a chart is a reference table of all the facts. If you want the reference kind, our free printable multiplication charts (1–12 and 1–100) are on the multiplication chart page — and each times table has its own printable-friendly page too.

Can I print the worksheets on A4 paper?

Yes. Both US Letter and A4 are supported — pick the paper size in the sample you download or in the custom generator, and the layout adjusts so nothing gets cut off.
