Refund Policy, in plain words

Short version

Every Supporter purchase is covered by a 14-day no-questions refund window — one-time purchases (including Lifetime) and the current period of a subscription. Subscriptions can also be cancelled anytime so nothing further is charged. If a paid feature doesn't work as described, we'll refund you no matter when you bought it.

What the 14-day window covers

  • 3-day trial ($1), monthly subscription (US$4.99), yearly subscription (US$29), and Lifetime purchase (US$89): within 14 days of a payment, you can ask for a full refund of that payment, for any reason.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Cancelling stops future charges and keeps access until the end of the period you have already paid for — no refund is needed for periods after cancellation. If a renewal charge lands and you'd rather have it back, the 14-day window applies to it too.
  • You don't need to give a reason within the window. It helps us improve if you share one, but it's never required.

If something doesn't work as described

This one has no time limit. If Live Race multiplayer or printable worksheets don't work as described on the pricing page — a real fault on our side, not a device or connection issue — tell us and we'll either fix it or refund the purchase, whenever you bought it.

How to request a refund

  • Self-service (fastest): open the Parent Center and click Manage subscription & refunds. This takes you to our payment provider's billing portal, where you can submit a refund request for your order.
  • By email: write to hi@tangyuanmath.com with the email address of your account (and the order number, if you have it). We'll take it from there.

We review requests within 1–2 business days. Approved refunds are executed by Waffo Pancake, our payment provider and merchant of record; the money usually returns to your payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on the method.

What a refund changes

  • A refund removes the access granted by that purchase: the Supporter features unlocked by that order (Live Race, custom printable worksheets) lock again when the refund completes.
  • Everything free stays free: practice games, charts, tips, and the rest of the site are unaffected.
  • Access from other sources is untouched: a redeem code, a gift, or a support grant you received separately keeps working.

Fair-use limits

A family that repeatedly buys and refunds may be declined further no-questions refunds — we'd still always refund a genuine fault. And if you're ever unhappy, please write to us before opening a chargeback with your bank: chargebacks are slow for you and costly for a small site, and we resolve direct requests faster.
